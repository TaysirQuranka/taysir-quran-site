Privacy Policy

Taysir Quran respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Effective Date: May 18, 2026 App Name: Taysir Quran Website: https://taysirquran.com

Taysir Quran respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when users use Taysir Quran to learn Quran reading through lessons, word focus, listening practice, and writing practice.

1. Information We Collect

Taysir Quran is designed to provide Quran reading practice without requiring users to create an account.

At this time, Taysir Quran does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, contacts, user voice recordings, or personal messages.

2. Children’s Privacy

Taysir Quran may be used by children, families, and beginners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The app is designed to help learners practice Quran reading without requiring personal data from the student. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it if needed.

3. App Progress and Local Data

Taysir Quran may store basic learning progress or app preferences locally on the user’s device. This may include selected language, theme preference, lesson progress, and recently used learning activities.

This information is used only to improve the learning experience inside the app. It is not used to personally identify the user.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Taysir Quran may offer monthly or yearly subscription plans to access premium learning content or features.

All subscription payments are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Taysir Quran does not directly collect or store credit card numbers, bank information, or payment details.

We may receive limited subscription-related information from Apple or Google, such as whether a subscription is active, expired, canceled, or renewed, in order to provide access to paid features.

5. Audio and Learning Content

Taysir Quran includes Quran reading lessons, audio, listening practice, and writing practice.

The app does not record the user’s voice or collect audio from the device. If a future version adds voice recording or pronunciation feedback, we will update this Privacy Policy and request permission before using the microphone.

6. Third-Party Services

Taysir Quran does not sell user data. At launch, Taysir Quran does not use advertising networks.

If future versions include analytics, account creation, crash reporting, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what data is collected and why.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers.

We may share limited information only when necessary to provide subscription access through Apple or Google, comply with legal requirements, or protect the safety, security, or integrity of the app.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and any information related to its operation. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need support, please contact us:

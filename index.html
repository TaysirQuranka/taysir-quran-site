About Taysir Quran

Taysir Quran is a Quran-centered learning app designed to help children and beginners learn to read the Quran with clarity, confidence, and love.

Many students spend months working through human-made preparatory books before they are introduced to real Quranic text. Taysir Quran takes a different path. It brings learners directly to the Book of Allah from the beginning, teaching Arabic letters, sounds, words, and reading skills through words that come from the Quran itself.

Every word students practice is taken from the Quran. They are given multiple opportunities to master each word through step-by-step lessons, focused word practice, listening activities, and writing exercises. Students see the word, hear it, repeat it, recognize it in Quranic context, and practice writing it until it becomes familiar.

The goal is to help students learn Quran reading in an easy, engaging, and meaningful way, while keeping the Quran at the center of their learning.

Taysir Quran teaches from the Quran, through the Quran, and toward confident Quran reading.
